# The Backrooms

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.11 that adds Level 0 of the Backrooms.

- **Getting in:** reality is wearing thin. At any moment a block near you can start glitching: a see-through, flickering overlay of static, color-split lines and torn yellow wallpaper appears over it, then fades. Sometimes two blocks one above the other glitch together and tear open, leaving a 1x2 doorway of static where the blocks were. Step into it and you fall through into the Backrooms. The blocks come back when the glitch ends. Suffocating inside a block in the overworld also has a 1-in-5 chance of noclipping you in.
- **Level 0:** an endless maze of mono-yellow wallpaper, moist carpet and humming, flickering fluorescent lights. It has open pillar halls, cramped corridors and dark zones full of dead lights. The blocks can't be broken in survival.
- **Smilers:** faces in the dark: two glowing eyes and a grin full of teeth, nothing else. There are two kinds that behave the same: the Smiler, with a fan of teeth that glow and split into red and blue at the edges, and the VHS Smiler, a fuzzy grey grin with round staring eyes. They never chase you. After about 20 seconds in Level 0 they start appearing where the lights are dead, always out of sight, and turn to watch you. Walk within 3 blocks of one and it lunges. If light reaches one, or you hit it, it slips away to another dark spot. Well-lit areas are safe. They don't appear on Peaceful.
- **The Bacteria:** three blocks of black wire: thin, knotted limbs, a crossbar of shoulders with curling tendrils, a dark blob of a head with a brim around it, and arms that reach the floor. It never stops shaking. After about a minute in Level 0 one can turn up a few rooms away. It wanders, and when it sees you it runs you down. Unlike a Smiler, it can be killed, but it takes a lot of hitting.
- **Getting out:** exit doors (green EXIT sign) are rare, hidden in the walls. Using one returns you to the surface where you fell through.

All blocks are in the Building Blocks creative tab. The Smilers and the Bacteria have spawn eggs in the Spawn Eggs tab. Glitched Blocks are in the Building Blocks tab: stack two and walk in to test the way in. A Smiler placed somewhere bright moves to a dark spot nearby, or vanishes if there is none. They don't attack players in creative.

Build with `gradle build`. The jar ends up in `build/libs/`.
