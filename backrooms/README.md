# The Backrooms

A Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.11 that adds Level 0 of the Backrooms.

- **Getting in:** when you suffocate inside a block in the overworld, there's a 1-in-5 chance you noclip out of reality instead of taking damage.
- **Level 0:** an endless maze of mono-yellow wallpaper, moist carpet and humming, flickering fluorescent lights. It has open pillar halls, cramped corridors and dark zones full of dead lights. The blocks can't be broken in survival.
- **The Smiler:** after about 45 seconds a tall, grinning figure starts hunting you. It only moves when nobody can see it. Look straight at it under a working light and it freezes. If you look away, or the lights around it die, it comes for you, and lights go out as it passes. Hitting it only makes it vanish and come back. It doesn't spawn on Peaceful.
- **Getting out:** exit doors (green EXIT sign) are rare, hidden in the walls. Using one returns you to the surface where you fell through.

All blocks are in the Building Blocks creative tab. Summon the monster with `/summon backrooms:smiler`.

Build with `gradle build`. The jar ends up in `build/libs/`.
