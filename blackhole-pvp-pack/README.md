# Black Hole PvP

A black, white and purple PvP resource pack for Minecraft 1.21.4 – 1.21.11. Every texture is original and drawn by `generate.py`.

**What it changes**
- Swords and axes: diamond is purple, netherite is black with a violet edge, iron is white
- Ender pearl, golden apples, totem (a small black hole), bow and arrow
- Low purple "void fire", so fire covers less of your screen
- Obsidian, crying obsidian, a thin cobweb, and end crystals with a black hole core
- Diamond armour, shield, purple enchant glint
- Hearts, hotbar, a ring crosshair, a black hole sun and a starfield End sky

**Install:** download `BlackHolePvP.zip` from the `blackhole-pack-latest` release (or run `python3 generate.py`, which needs Pillow and numpy). Put it in `.minecraft/resourcepacks` and turn it on in Options → Resource Packs.
